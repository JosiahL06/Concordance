# Tour of the screens

Concordance has four screens. The rest of this guide refers back to them by
name, so it is worth a minute to learn what each one is for.

| Screen | What it is for | Main areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Start a new round, reopen one you have not finished, change the theme or haptics | **START A NEW ROUND**, the **Resume round** list, the buttons along the top |
| **Setup** | Choose the rules, the teams, the quizzers, and how the scores look | The ruleset tabs, the team and quizzer fields, the Modern/Classic cards |
| **Live scoring** | Where you keep score during the round | The team scoreboards, the question navigator, the bottom console |
| **Summary** | The final result, the announcements, and the export | Team totals, per-quizzer totals, the export buttons |

The **Home** screen also has three small buttons in its top corner: a switch for
light and dark, a switch for the tap vibration, and an **About** button that
shows the app's version. See [Settings](../guide/settings.md).

## How the screens connect

A round always moves forward in one direction:

**Home → Setup → Live scoring → Summary**

You can go back at any point, and going back is always safe: every ruling you
have made is already saved by the time the screen changes. The **Done** button
on the summary returns you to the home screen.

## The two live scoring screens

The live screen comes in two versions, **Modern** and **Classic**. They are two
ways of looking at the same round, not two different apps, so switching between
them never changes a score or a ruling.

- **Modern** splits the screen into two halves — red on the left, green on the
  right — with a large card for each seated quizzer.
- **Classic** shows a grid like the paper scoresheet: one row per quizzer, one
  column per question.

There is more on both in [Modern vs Classic](../explanation/modern-vs-classic.md).

## The bottom console

The console is the strip of large buttons along the bottom of the live screen,
and it is the only place where scoring happens. **CORRECT**, **INCORRECT**,
**FOUL**, **Undo**, and **Interruption** all live here, along with the **More**
menu and the **Summary** button.

One thing is worth repeating: on the Classic screen, tapping a cell in the grid
moves you to that question, but it does not score it. Scoring always happens in
the console.

## The question navigator

The navigator is the row of question numbers on the live screen. It shows at a
glance what happened on each question, using the same marks the paper
scoresheet uses — a circle for an interrupted question, a slash for a contested
one, and an **F** for a foul. Tap a number to look at that question. See
[Question navigator](../guide/question-navigator.md).
