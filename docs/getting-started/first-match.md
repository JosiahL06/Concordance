**Goal:** score one practice round from start to finish.

## Outline

1. **Open the app** — what you see first (home screen), where "Start a new
   round" and "Resume round" live.
2. **Pick a ruleset** — TBQ vs JBQ tabs; what the details card tells you.
3. **Name the teams and quizzers** — Red/Green defaults, seated vs bench.
4. **Choose a live view** — Modern or Classic (link: Modern vs Classic).
5. **Score a few questions** — set value, tap Correct/Incorrect, watch the
   running score change.
6. **Make a mistake on purpose and undo it** — the single most important habit
   the tutorial teaches.
7. **Record a foul and a time-out** — just enough to know the buttons exist.
8. **Finish the round** — Summary screen, PDF/CSV export.
9. **Close the app mid-round and reopen it** — the round is still there
   (autosave is the feature that matters most; prove it in the tutorial).
