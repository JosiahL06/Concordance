# Your first match

This walkthrough takes you through one practice round, from opening the app to
closing it again. Follow it in order — there are no choices to make along the
way. It takes about ten minutes.

**Before you start:** Concordance needs to be installed. If it is not, see
[Which download do I need?](../installation/index.md).

## 1. Open the app

The first screen is the home screen. It has one large button, **START A NEW
ROUND**, and a **Resume round** list underneath it. The list is empty until you
have a round you have not finished.

## 2. Pick the rules

Tap **START A NEW ROUND**. The setup screen opens. Two tabs sit at the top:
**Teen Bible Quiz** and **Junior Bible Quiz**. Pick either one to practice
with. A card under the tabs updates to show that ruleset — how many questions
there are, what they are worth, and the numbers for quizzing out, striking out,
and fouling out.

## 3. Name the teams and quizzers

Each team starts with the name of its side of the quiz box: **Red** and
**Green**. You can leave those names as they are, or type your own. If you
clear a field by mistake, the app falls back to the default for you.

Under each team name is a row for its quizzers. **Seated** is how many quizzers
sit at the table; **Bench** is how many extras are waiting to rotate in. Use
**+** and **−** to change them. Every quizzer gets a name field, already filled
with a sensible default like `Red 1` or `Green 2` — type over them or leave
them alone.

## 4. Choose how the scores look

Below the teams are two cards, **Modern** and **Classic**. Both keep score in
exactly the same way; they only look different. **Modern** splits the screen
into a red half and a green half, with a large card for each quizzer.
**Classic** looks like the paper scoresheet, with one row per quizzer and one
column per question. Pick one to try — you can change it later. If you want to
know more before choosing, read [Modern vs Classic](../explanation/modern-vs-classic.md).

## 5. Start the round

Tap **START ROUND**. The live scoring screen appears. From this moment on,
everything you do is saved the moment you do it.

## 6. Score a few questions

Before a question can be scored, Concordance needs to know what it is worth.
Tap the value row for the current question, or the **SET VALUE** button at the
top of the screen, and choose **10**, **20**, or **30**.

Then tap the quizzer who answered, and tap **CORRECT** or **INCORRECT**. The
team's total and that quizzer's own score change straight away. A correct answer
moves you on to the next question by itself.

## 7. Make a mistake on purpose

Tap **INCORRECT** for a quizzer you meant to mark correct. Then tap **UNDO**.
Everything goes back the way it was — the score, the quizzer's counts, and the
position on the screen. This is the most useful button in the app, so get
comfortable with it now. See [Undo & corrections](../guide/undo-and-corrections.md).

## 8. Record a foul and a time-out

Tap **FOUL**, then choose the quizzer who fouled. A foul takes points off that
quizzer; a team foul, which does not need a quizzer selected, takes points off the team
total.

To take a time-out, use the red or green time-out button beside each team's
name. That team's count goes up. In a real match you would also record
interruptions and contests — those have their own button and menu.

## 9. Finish the round

When the last question is done, tap **Summary** in the bottom bar. The summary
shows the final score, each quizzer's totals, and the announcements the ruleset
asks for. From here you can save the round as a PDF or a spreadsheet file, and
share it if you like. Tap **Done** to return to the home screen. See
[Match summary](../guide/match-summary.md).

## 10. Prove that it saves

Close the app completely — do not just press Home. Now open it again. Your
round is waiting under **Resume round**. Open it and you are exactly where you
left off, on the same screen with the same score. That is the autosave at work,
and it means a dead battery or a crash mid-match will not cost you the round.
See [Autosave & recovery](../guide/autosave-and-recovery.md).
