# FAQ

## About the app

### Is Concordance an official Bible Quiz app?

No. It is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by
Bible Quiz, and it is not the scorekeeper of record for official matches.
Personal, practice, and unofficial use is welcome.

### Who is it for?


### Does it need the internet?

No, not at any point. It never connects to anything. See
[Offline-only, no accounts](../explanation/offline-only.md).

### Will it work on my device?

It runs on Amazon Fire tablets, other Android tablets and phones, Windows,
Linux, and Mac. Find your device in the table on
[Which download do I need?](../installation/index.md).

### Is it free? What is its license?

Yes, it is free. It is open source under the AGPL-3.0-only license.

### Does it work on an iPad?

Not yet. The toolkit the app is built with does not currently support iPads, so
it is not something an upcoming release is likely to add soon.

## Using it

### Can I run a whole tournament with it?

Not yet. One round at a time is what the app does today; running a schedule or
several matches at once is not part of it.

### Does it show the questions?

No. It records what happens on each question — correct, incorrect, interrupted,
contested — but it does not display question text or tracks.

### Can I fix a mistake?

Yes. Tap **Undo**, which takes back the most recent ruling. See
[Undo & corrections](../guide/undo-and-corrections.md).

### Will it lose my round if the battery dies?

No. Everything is saved as you go. See
[Autosave & recovery](../guide/autosave-and-recovery.md).

### Can I use it alongside the paper scoresheet?

Yes. It records the same marks the paper sheet does, so it works well as a
backup or a second copy. See
[Question navigator](../guide/question-navigator.md).

### Why is there no big scoreboard for the room?

Concordance is a scorekeeper's tool, not a display. It shows the score to the
person keeping it, and it does not add a projector or audience view.

### Does it make any sound?

No, never. That is deliberate: a match should not be disturbed by a tablet's
noises. See [Settings](../guide/settings.md).

## Rules & rulesets

### Which rulebook versions are built in?

Teen Bible Quiz (2025-26) and Junior Bible Quiz (2026). See
[Rulesets](../rulesets/index.md).

### My league uses different numbers. Can I change them?

Yes, by adding a ruleset file; no changes to the app are needed. See
[Ruleset schema](../design/ruleset-schema.md).

### Where do the rules come from, and why are they not quoted here?

They come from the official rulebooks, which are copyrighted and licensed for
local church use only, so this wiki does not reproduce them. It lists only the
numbers the app needs. See [Rulesets](../rulesets/index.md).

## Helping out

### I found a bug. What do you need from me?

Open an issue on the
[project's issues page](https://github.com/JosiahL06/Concordance/issues).
Include your device, the app version (from the **About** button), what you
tapped, and a screenshot if you can.

Yes, contributions are welcome. See the
[contributing guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
