# Set up a match

**Goal:** get from the home screen to the live scoring screen with the right
rules and the right people in the right seats.

## Start a new round

On the home screen, tap **START A NEW ROUND**. The **Resume round** list just
below it is for rounds you have already begun and not finished — tapping one of
those rows takes you straight back into that round. This page is about starting
a fresh one.

## Pick the rules

Two tabs sit at the top of the setup screen: **Teen Bible Quiz** and **Junior
Bible Quiz**. Tap the one you are playing.

A card under the tabs then describes the rules of that choice — how many
questions there are and what they are worth, and the numbers for quizzing out,
striking out, and fouling out. It also lists the limits for time-outs and
contests. A quick read is a good way to confirm you have the right one.

The rules come from the season's ruleset, not from the match. If your league
uses different numbers, that is a new ruleset rather than a setting here — see
[Rulesets](../rulesets/index.md).

## Name the teams

By default each team carries the name of its side of the quiz box: **Red** and
**Green**. You can keep those names or type your own — many scorekeepers enter
the actual team or church names, which makes the summary easier to read later.
If you clear a box by accident, the app puts the default back.

## Set out the quizzers

Each team has two numbers to set:

- **Seated** — how many quizzers sit at the table. Teen Bible Quiz allows up to
  three seated quizzers and Junior Bible Quiz up to four; every team needs at
  least one.
- **Bench** — how many extra quizzers are waiting to rotate in.

Use the **+** and **−** buttons to change each count. A name box appears for
every quizzer, seated or on the bench. Each one is filled in with a sensible
default such as `Red 1` or `Green 2`; type over any of them, or leave them if
you do not yet have the real names.

The order of the names matters. The first seated quizzer is Red 1, the second is
Red 2, and so on. The scoreboard and the summary follow that order, so enter the
names in the order the quizzers are actually sitting.

## Choose how the scores look

Two cards, **Modern** and **Classic**, choose the layout of the live screen.
Both keep score the same way. Your device remembers the last one you picked, but
you can choose a different one just for this round here. See
[Modern vs Classic](../explanation/modern-vs-classic.md) and
[Settings](settings.md).

## Start the round

Tap **START ROUND**. The live scoring screen opens. Everything you set on this
screen is saved as the round begins, and from here on every tap is saved too.

## Common mistakes

- **The same name twice.** Two quizzers may share a name, but it makes the
  summary hard to read — give them something to tell them apart.
- **Too few or too many seated quizzers.** This changes the scoreboard, so set it
  before you start if you can. You can still swap quizzers in during the round.
- **The wrong ruleset.** The ruleset sets the point values and the limits, so a
  wrong one will score the match differently. Check the details card before you
  tap START ROUND.
