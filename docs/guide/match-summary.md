# Match summary

**Goal:** close out the round and keep a record of it.

## Getting to the summary

The **Summary** button in the bottom console comes to life once the match is
complete. You can also open the summary from the strip that appears at the end
of the round. Tapping **Done** on the summary returns you to the home screen.

## What the summary shows

- The final result — which team won, or that it was a tie — with both team
  totals.
- A breakdown for each quizzer: their score, their numbers of correct and
  incorrect answers, and their fouls.
- Each team's time-outs taken and challenges recorded.
- A question-by-question review, with tags for interrupted and contested
  questions.
- The end-of-round announcements the rulesets call for.

## Saving a copy

Two buttons save the round as a file:

- **PDF** — a tidy, printable score sheet. It has the team totals, a table of
  each team's quizzers, and a question-by-question list, and it is laid out to be
  easy to read on paper.
- **Spreadsheet** — a plain file with one row per quizzer and one row per
  question, for anyone keeping records in a spreadsheet program.

Either button opens your device's usual sharing window, so you choose where the
file goes: email it, save it to a folder, send it to a printer, or share it with
another app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the file only goes where you send
it.

On a tablet the sharing window may offer fewer places to send the file than on a
computer. If you are not sure where a saved file ended up, share it to an app
you recognise, such as your email or a file storage app.

## Keeping the round without saving a copy

You do not have to save anything. The round stays on the device under
**Resume round** until you delete it, so you can open the summary again later.
