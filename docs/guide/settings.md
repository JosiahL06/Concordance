# Settings

**Goal:** adjust the app for the device and the room it is used in.

There is no separate settings screen. The few settings that exist are small
switches in the top corner of the home screen, plus the live view that the app
remembers from your last round. Everything is saved on the device itself.

## The switches in the top corner

Three small buttons sit in the top corner of the home screen:

- A **light/dark switch**. Tapping it moves between a light view, a dark view,
  and following whatever the tablet is set to. By default the app follows the
  tablet. The red and green team colors stay clear and easy to tell apart in
  both light and dark.
- A **vibration switch**. This turns the small tap vibration on or off. It is
  **off by default**, so a fresh tablet cannot buzz or distract anyone during a
  match. Turn it on if you would like the confirmation when you score.
- An **About** button. This shows the version of the app you are running, along
  with the license information.

## The live view the app remembers

When you start a round and choose **Modern** or **Classic** on the setup screen,
the app remembers that choice for next time. It is remembered per device,
because the same tablet is often passed between scorekeepers. You can still pick
the other one for a single round without changing the remembered default — just
choose it again on the setup screen. See
[Modern vs Classic](../explanation/modern-vs-classic.md).

## Sound

There is no sound setting, because the app never plays any sound at all. That is
deliberate: an official match should never be interrupted by a tablet's noises.
The app makes no beeps, clicks, or music, and it does not rely on any.

## Accessibility

The app is made to be used by touch and to work with a screen reader. The
buttons are large, and nothing is hidden behind a long press or a swipe. Notices
appear as ordinary text, so a screen reader can read them aloud. See
[Alerts](alerts.md).

## Where settings are kept

Settings are stored on the device only. They are not part of a round, and they
are not shared between devices. Reinstalling the app resets them to their
defaults.

## The settings at a glance

| Setting | Choices | Default | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light or dark | Light · Dark · Follow the tablet | Follow the tablet | This device |
| Tap vibration | On · Off | Off | This device |
| Live view | Modern · Classic | Modern | This device, remembered from your last round |
