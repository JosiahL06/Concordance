**Goal:** record everything besides correct/incorrect answers.

## To cover

- **Interruptions** — what an interruption is in quiz terms; how to record it
  on the answer, and the navigator glyph (circled) that shows it happened.
- **Fouls** — personal foul vs team foul; the `−5` deduction; that the foul mark
  survives even if the question is later voided (D3); the `F` glyph in the
  navigator; third foul ⇒ foul-out and the alert.
- **Contests / Coach's Appeals** — one unified *challenge* tally per team (D2);
  success/failure recording; the per-team limit notification (TBQ: 3rd
  unsuccessful; JBQ: 2 appeals).
- **Time-outs** — taking one, the count for each team, and the notification at
  the limit (TBQ 3, 4th is denied and penalised as a team foul — the app
  deliberately does NOT auto-assess that foul, it prompts the keeper); JBQ
  overtime carries time-outs over *and* grants one extra.
- **Voiding a question** — what a void retracts (answer points and the
  correct/incorrect counts, re-evaluating quiz-out/strike-out) and what it
  leaves alone (fouls); substitutes occupy the same question number (D4).
- A summary table: *action → button → navigator mark → what changes*.
