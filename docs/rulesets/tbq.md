# TBQ preset (Teen Bible Quiz, 25-26)

**Ruleset id:** `tbq-25-26` → [`assets/rulesets/tbq-25-26.json`](https://github.com/JosiahL06/Concordance/blob/main/assets/rulesets/tbq-25-26.json)

Teen Bible Quiz is one of the two rulesets built into Concordance. This page
lists the numbers it uses to keep score.

## The match

- **Two teams.**
- **Twenty questions**: eight worth 10 points, nine worth 20, and three worth
  30.
- **Seated quizzers**: at least one and at most three per team. There is no
  limit on how many extra quizzers a team may keep on the bench.

## Answers

- **Correct:** the full value of the question.
- **Incorrect:** half the value taken off — a 30-point question answered wrong
  is −15.

## Quizzing out, striking out, fouling out

- **Quiz out:** a quizzer's fifth correct answer. It adds a **+20** bonus. The
  quizzer stays in the match.
- **Strike out:** a quizzer's third incorrect answer.
- **Foul:** −5. A quizzer's third foul is a foul out. A foul charged to the team
  itself (a coach's foul, for example) takes 5 off the team total.

## Time-outs and contests

- **Time-outs:** three per team in regulation. A fourth request is not counted,
  and a team foul is owed — the app tells you, and you record the foul.
- **Contests:** recorded as successful or unsuccessful. The app notifies the
  scorekeeper on a team's third unsuccessful contest, and refuses any further
  contests from that team. Up to two contests per question per team.

## Overtime

If the match is level after twenty questions, overtime begins with 10-point
questions, taken one at a time until the tie is broken. No time-outs may be
taken in overtime.

## How TBQ differs from JBQ

| | Teen Bible Quiz | Junior Bible Quiz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Questions | 20 | 20 |
| Value mix | eight 10s, nine 20s, three 30s | ten 10s, seven 20s, three 30s |
| Quiz out | 5th correct, +20 bonus | 6th correct, +10 bonus |
| On quizzing out | Stays in the match | Leaves the match |
| Strike out | 3rd incorrect | 3rd incorrect |
| Challenge | Contest | Coach's Appeal |
| Challenge limit | 3rd unsuccessful notifies | 2 per team |
| Seated quizzers | up to 3 | up to 4 |
| Overtime time-outs | none | leftover time-outs plus one |

!!! warning "Rulebooks govern"
    These are the numbers the preset uses. The official TBQ rulebook is the
    authority, and nothing on this page restates it.
