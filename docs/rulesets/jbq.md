## Outline

- **Answers** — correct: + full value. Incorrect: − half.
- **Quiz out** — 6th correct answer ⇒ +10 bonus, and the quizzer **leaves the
  match**.
- **Strike out** — 3rd incorrect answer, and the quizzer leaves.
- **Fouls** — −5, marked `F`; 3 fouls ⇒ foul out. Other-person fouls are
  assessed to the team (−5).
- **Time-outs** — notified at the limit; overtime carries remaining time-outs
  over *and* grants one extra (cap 3 + 1 = 4).
- **Coach's Appeals** — 2 per team maximum; recorded with success/failure.
- **Comparison table** vs TBQ (thresholds, bonus, departure on out).
- What is identical to TBQ, stated once so the page does not repeat it.
