# Concordance

**A clean, modern scorekeeping app for Bible Quiz matches**, built to be
intuitive and usable within seconds of picking it up.

!!! warning "Not for official use"
    Concordance is an independent community project, **not affiliated with or
    endorsed by Bible Quiz** or its organizing bodies, and it is **not the
    scorekeeper of record** for official matches. Personal, practice, and
    unofficial use is welcome.

## Outline

- One-paragraph "what is this" (two teams, quiz box, questions of varying
  value, points for correct answers, quiz-outs/strike-outs/foul-outs).
- What makes it different: one-tap scoring, prominent undo, every mark on the
  paper scoresheet captured digitally, never makes a sound, works with no
  internet, autosaves every action.
- Screenshots table (Setup · Modern · Classic · Summary) — reuse
  `screenshots/setup.png` etc.
- Supported rulesets: TBQ 25-26 and JBQ 2026 built in.
