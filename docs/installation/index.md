- Where downloads come from: the project **Releases** page (link).
- The recommended device and why: Amazon Fire HD 10, landscape, no Google Play
  services needed — the APK is GMS-free by design.
- Every release also ships `SHA256SUMS`: [verify your download](verifying-downloads.md).
- Desktop builds are currently **unsigned** → expect a first-launch trust
  warning (each OS page explains the exact click path).
- What you need before you start: about 1 minute, no account, no internet
  required after install.
