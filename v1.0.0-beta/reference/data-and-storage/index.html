Data & storage¶

Goal: answer "where is my data, and who can see it?"

What is kept¶

Two things:

  • Your rounds. Each round, with every ruling you recorded in it, is kept so it can be opened again later. The scores are worked out from those rulings rather than stored on their own.
  • Your settings. The light/dark choice, the tap-vibration choice, and the last live view you used.

Nothing else is stored. There are no accounts, no usage tracking, and no information about you.

Where it is kept¶

Rounds are kept in a single file called concordance.db, in the app's own folder on your device. The exact folder depends on the platform:

Platform Where the file is
Android / Fire The app's private storage, not visible in a normal file manager
Windows %APPDATA%\Concordance\Concordance\concordance.db
Linux ~/.local/share/org.concordance.concordance/concordance.db
macOS ~/Library/Application Support/org.concordance.app/concordance.db

Your settings are kept separately, in the place your device normally keeps an app's small preferences.

How it is written¶

Your rulings are written to the file as you make them. Each change is written in one piece, so if the tablet loses power in the middle of a write, the round is left at the state it was in before that change — never half-applied.

When you reopen a round, the scores are worked out again from the rulings on record. That is what makes undo exact, as described in Autosave & recovery.

What is not kept¶

  • No account, and no sign-in of any kind.
  • No names of real people, beyond the team and quizzer names you type in yourself.
  • No usage tracking, no analytics, and no crash reports.
  • No network activity at all — the app never connects to the internet.

Keeping a copy¶

Saving a round as a PDF or a spreadsheet file is the supported way to keep a copy, and it is the only way we recommend. On a desktop computer you can also copy the concordance.db file itself, but that is not supported and a copy cannot be loaded back in — it is there for emergencies only.

Deleting data¶

There are three levels, each with a different effect:

What you do What is removed
Delete one round from the home screen That round only. You are asked to confirm first.
Uninstall the app All rounds and your settings
Clear the app's storage (Android/Fire) All rounds and your settings

Uninstalling removes your rounds for good, so save anything you care about first. See Updating & uninstalling.
